Birdle

Pick a birding region. Each board hides some of its signature birds among the words.

    Hints

    How to play

    Find words by connecting letters: up, down, sideways or diagonally. Swipe across the letters, or type the word and press Enter.

    • Words are at least 4 letters long.
    • Each square can be used only once per word.
    • A few of the words are birds of the region. Find them all!
    • Rarer words count as bonus words.
    • Squares fade once every word that uses them is found.

    Longer words are worth more points. As you fill the progress bar you earn help:

    • ★ First star: a red number shows how many words start on each square.
    • ★ Second star: a white number shows how many words use each square.
    • ★ Third star: hints in the word list let you see first letters and sort the words alphabetically.
