Birdle

Pick a region and a level, from a quick 3×3 Express to a 7×7 Extreme Birder. Each board hides some of the region's birds among the words.

    How to play

    Find words by connecting neighbouring letters: up, down, sideways or diagonally.

    WAT ORS LNE

    Swipe across the letters, or type the word and press Enter. Words need at least 4 letters, and each square can be used once per word.

    Find the birds

    Every board is set in a birding region, and a few of its words are that region's birds.

    Birds are worth double points. Tap the word count above the board to see your birds and words, and how many letters each missing bird has.

    Rarer words count as bonus words but don't add points.

    Earn stars

    Longer words score more. Stars on the progress bar earn you help:

    R37

    ★ First star: earned by the points for all the board's birds, so find them first! A red number shows how many words start on each square.

    ★ Second star: a white number shows how many words use each square.

    A square fades once every word that uses it is found.

    Hints

    ★ Third star: hints appear above the word list.

    Show some letters reveals the start (and, for long words, the end) of each word you haven't found yet.

    Sort words alphabetically puts the missing words in order among the ones you've found.

    💡 Reveals light up a 4, 5 or 6 letter word on the board for you to swipe. You get one per board, plus one for finding all the birds.
